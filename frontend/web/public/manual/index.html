1. Getting started

UCYSSPass is a ticketing and venue booking system for cybersecurity events such as CTFs, bootcamps, conferences and workshops. Customers book seats and pay in a sandbox, organisers create events and ticket tiers, and administrators look after users, venues and every record. Our group built one shared API and four different frontends on top of it.

The first load can be slow

The API runs on Render's free plan, which goes to sleep when nobody uses it. The first request after a quiet spell can take up to a minute while the server wakes up. After that, everything is fast.

  • Aqqwa's and Aijaz's apps show a banner that starts with "Waking up the server" while they wait.
  • Hakim's web app shows grey loading placeholders until the data arrives.
  • Erfan's mobile app shows loading placeholders and waits up to 45 seconds before it shows a message.

Running the mobile app

Erfan's app has no public download. To try it, run it with Expo from the repository (these are the steps in the project README):

  1. Open a terminal in frontend/ucyss-mobile.
  2. Run npm install.
  3. Copy .env.example to .env. It already points the app at the shared API on Render.
  4. Run npx expo start and open the app on a phone or an emulator.

2. Roles at a glance

Every request is checked by the API, so a page that is hidden in the menu is also refused by the server.

RoleWhat they can do
Guest (not signed in)Create a customer account and sign in. In Hakim's web app a guest can also browse events and open an event page. The other apps ask you to sign in first.
CustomerBrowse events, check availability, make or cancel eligible bookings, and view their own booking details and status.
OrganiserCreate and manage events, schedules and ticket availability, and view the bookings for their own events.
AdministratorManage users and venues, and oversee system records: every event and every booking.

3. Customer guide

Sign in as customer@sentrypass.test to follow these steps.

Hakim's web app

The public site has Events and My passes in the top menu. You can browse without signing in.

Browse and filter events

  1. Click Events in the top menu.
  2. Type a word in the search box ("Search events, for example web security") and click Search.
  3. Click a category: All, CTF, Bootcamp, Conference or Workshop.
  4. Pick a date range in the Any date list (Next 7 days, Next 30 days) or a price in the Any price list (Free, Up to RM 50, Up to RM 100).
  5. Click Clear filters to start again. Use Previous and Next to see more pages.

Each card shows the price ("Free" or "From RM ...") and the seats, for example "12 seats left", "Only 3 seats left" or "Sold out, join the waitlist".

Open an event and check seats left

  1. Click an event card.
  2. Read the date, the venue or "online meeting", and the description.
  3. Look under Choose your pass. Each tier shows its price and "X of Y seats left", or "Sold out".
  4. Share the event with Share on WhatsApp or Copy link if you like.

Free cancellation is allowed until 24 hours before the event. After that, tickets are not refundable.

Book a free tier

  1. Open the event.
  2. Click Book this pass on a free tier. If you are not signed in, you are sent to Sign in and brought back afterwards.
  3. Read the green message: "You're in. Your ... pass is confirmed and waiting in My passes."

When a tier is sold out the button says Join waitlist. You are confirmed and emailed automatically if a seat opens. A Members only tier can only be booked by UCYSS members.

Book a paid tier with the sandbox payment

  1. Click Book this pass on a paid tier.
  2. Watch the Complete your booking window. A timer shows how long "your seat is held".
  3. Choose a method under Pay with: Card, Online banking or E-wallet.
  4. Leave Sandbox test result on Payment succeeds. Choose Payment is declined or Insufficient funds to see a failed payment.
  5. Click Pay RM ....
  6. Read "Paid. You are in." and click View my pass.

No real money is used. Click Release the seat to give the seat back. If you close the window, the seat stays held until the timer ends, and you can finish from My passes with Pay now. When the timer runs out you see Hold ended.

Pick a numbered seat

  1. Open an event with numbered seats. A seat map of the room appears above the tiers.
  2. Use the zoom buttons on the map if the seats are small.
  3. Click a free seat. The tier's button changes from Choose a seat above to Book seat A5 (with your seat's label).
  4. Click Book seat ..., then pay if the tier is paid.

If someone else takes the seat first you see "Seat ... was just taken. Pick another one."

View My passes and their status

  1. Click My passes.
  2. Read each pass: the event, the venue or "Online meeting", the date, the seat and the status tag (Confirmed, Pending, Waitlisted, Checked in or Cancelled).
  3. Check the payment line, for example "Paid RM 30.00" or "Refunded RM 30.00". A waitlisted pass says "You're number N in the queue."

Cancel a booking

  1. Click My passes.
  2. Click Cancel booking on the pass.
  3. Read the browser's question. It tells you whether you will be refunded, then click OK.
  4. Read "Booking cancelled." or "Booking cancelled. RM ... has been refunded."

Extras: QR pass, calendar, online meetings, certificate and profile

  • QR pass: click Show pass on a confirmed in-person pass. A large QR code opens for the door scanner. Click Done to close it.
  • Calendar: click Add to calendar to download a calendar file (.ics) for the event.
  • Online meeting: a confirmed online pass has a join button such as Join on Zoom or Join meeting. Before the meeting opens, the button reads "Opens ..." with the time.
  • Certificate: after you are checked in and the event has ended, click Download certificate to save a PDF.
  • Profile: click your name at the top right. Change your details and click Save details, or fill in Change password and click Change password.
  • Forgotten password: click Forgot your password? on the sign-in page, click Send the code, then type the Six-digit code from the email and your new password, and click Change password.

4. Organiser guide

Sign in as aisyah@ucyss.test or farid@ucyss.test. An organiser only sees and changes their own events.

Hakim's web app

The organiser area has a side menu with My events and Check-in. On a phone, open it with the menu button at the top left.

Dashboard numbers

  1. Click My events.
  2. Read the tiles: Upcoming events, Tickets sold, Checked in and Net revenue.
  3. Read the Attendance panel to see how many registered guests really came to events that have started.

Create an event (in person or online)

  1. Click Create event.
  2. Choose In person or Online meeting.
  3. Fill in Title and Category.
  4. Choose a Venue (in person), or paste a Meeting link (online). The form shows which platform it detected, such as Zoom.
  5. Set Status, Starts, Ends and Description. For an in-person event, turn on Numbered seats if guests should choose their exact seat.
  6. Click Create event. The event is saved as a draft and its page opens.

Add, edit and delete ticket tiers

  1. Open the event from My events.
  2. Under Ticket tiers, click Add tier.
  3. Fill in Name, Price (RM) and Seats. Set Who can book to Anyone or UCYSS members only. A numbered-seat event also asks for Seats per row.
  4. Click Save tier.
  5. Click Edit on a tier to change it, or Delete and then Delete tier to remove it.

Publish, or move back to draft

  1. Open the event.
  2. Click Publish event at the top. Customers can see it and book straight away.
  3. Click Move back to draft to hide it again.

You can also use the actions menu on a row in My events: Publish or Move back to draft.

Edit or delete an event

  1. Open the event, change the fields and click Save event.
  2. To cancel it, set Status to Cancelled, click Save event and confirm with Cancel the event. Every active booking is cancelled, paid tickets are refunded and guests are emailed.
  3. Click Duplicate event to copy it, or Export attendees (CSV) to download the guest list.

The organiser area has no delete button. An administrator can delete an event from Events in the admin area.

View the bookings for an event

  1. Open the event.
  2. Scroll to Attendees to see each guest, tier, seat, booking time and status.
  3. For a numbered-seat event, look at Seat map to see who holds each seat.

Check guests in at the door

  1. Click Check-in in the side menu.
  2. Choose the event in the Event list.
  3. Click Start scanning and point the camera at the guest's QR pass.
  4. Read the result. "Cleared to enter" means the guest is checked in.
  5. Click Scan next guest.

If the camera does not work, open Can't scan? Enter the ticket instead, type the booking number and signature, and click Check in. A failed scan shows "Forged or altered ticket", "Already used, or not confirmed", "Not your event" or "Not a UCYSS ticket".

Send an announcement

  1. Open a published event and scroll to Announcements.
  2. Fill in Subject and Message.
  3. Click Send to N people and confirm with Send announcement. Everyone booked, and everyone on the waitlist, is emailed.

5. Administrator guide

Sign in as admin@sentrypass.test. Please do not change or delete the shared demo accounts, because the whole group uses them.

Hakim's web app

The admin side menu has Overview, Events, Bookings, Venues, People, Emails and Organiser tools.

Overview

  1. Click Overview.
  2. Read the tiles: Net revenue, Tickets sold, Seats held and Check-in rate.
  3. Read Upcoming events, Needs attention and Recent activity, then the charts lower down.

Manage users

  1. Click People. Filter with Everyone, Customers, Organisers or Admins, or search by name or email.
  2. To create an account, click Add account, fill in Full name, Email, Temporary password and Role, and click Add account.
  3. To change a role, open the row's actions menu, click Change role, pick the Role and click Save role.
  4. To delete, click Delete account in the menu and confirm.
  5. Click Add to member list to let someone book members-only tiers. Click Export CSV to download the list.

Your own account has no actions menu. This app does not lock the shared demo accounts, so take care with them.

Manage venues

  1. Click Venues.
  2. Click Add venue, fill in Name, Address and Capacity, and click Save venue.
  3. Use the row's menu to Edit or Delete a venue. A venue that still has events cannot be deleted.

All events

  1. Click Events. Filter with All, Published, Draft, Cancelled or Completed, the type list (All types, In person, Online) or the search box.
  2. Open a row's actions menu and choose Open, Publish, Move back to draft, Mark as completed, Cancel event or Delete.
  3. Click Open to see who created the event, How it's going, the tiers, the seat map and its bookings, with Export attendees (CSV).

All bookings

  1. Click Bookings.
  2. Filter with All, Confirmed, Waitlisted, Awaiting payment, Checked in or Cancelled.
  3. Use the row's menu to Cancel booking (the seat goes to the waitlist) or Delete record. Click Export CSV to download.

Extras: Emails log and Organiser tools

  • Emails: every booking email the platform tried to send, with the provider's answer. Filter with the Show list and open Raw response on a row.
  • Organiser tools: opens the organiser area so an admin can create and run events. Click Back to admin to return.

7. FAQ

Why can't I see the Admin menu?
Menus depend on your role. Only an administrator sees the admin pages. Sign out and sign in as admin@sentrypass.test.
Why is the first load slow?
The API is on a free server that sleeps when idle. The first request can take up to a minute. After that it is fast.
Why did my booking say pending?
You booked a paid tier. The seat is held for a few minutes while you pay. Pay before the timer ends, or the seat goes back on sale.
Why was I sent back to my home page?
You opened a page for another role. Each app sends you back to your own home. Aqqwa's app says "That page is for ...s only, so you were sent back to your own home." Aijaz's app says "That page is not available for your account, so we brought you back here."
Why am I on the waitlist?
The tier was sold out. If someone cancels, the next person in line is confirmed automatically.
Do I get my money back if I cancel?
Free cancellation is allowed until 24 hours before the event. After that, tickets are not refunded. All payments are sandbox payments, so no real money moves.
Why can't I change or delete a demo account?
Aqqwa's, Aijaz's and Erfan's apps lock the four shared demo accounts, because the whole group uses them in the demo video.
Do I need an account to look at events?
In Hakim's web app, no: anyone can browse and open events. Aqqwa's, Aijaz's and Erfan's apps ask you to sign in first.
